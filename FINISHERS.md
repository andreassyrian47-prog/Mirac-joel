HELLBOUND — Gravitas v0.20.0. Durations exclude the obstacle-checked entry alignment and the kill-beat slow-motion; "kill at" is the wall-clock moment the finishing blow lands (the strike beats before it carry hit-stop holds, and the remainder is the held final pose, the breath and the rise while the camera lingers on the body).

**V** opens the Grimoire. Equip on **E**, or shuffle separately through **40 standard / 7 boss / 2 Midget-exclusive** executions. Ordinary and small enemies require ≤60% vitality or broken will; bosses require ≤20%. Standard moves remain boss-compatible. Small-exclusive moves only use their own creature. Previews do not award kills or consume run resources.
